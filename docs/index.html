Your SUTD timetable,
in your calendar.

Reads your weekly schedule straight off the portal page you are already looking at, and gives you a calendar file for Google Calendar or Apple Calendar. No copying, no typing.

Coming to the Chrome Web Store

Waiting on Google's review. Until then you can install it manually — see below.

How it works

About a minute, start to finish.

  1. Log into the portal as normalIn whatever browser you already use. The extension never asks for your password.
  2. Open your Weekly ScheduleManage Classes → View My Weekly Schedule, showing the calendar grid.
  3. Click the extension iconA panel opens. Press Scan and leave the tab alone while it reads each week.
  4. Download and importYou get a SUTD_Calendar.ics file. The panel shows you exactly how to add it to Google Calendar.

What it handles

Real dates, not guessesReads every week of the term as the portal actually renders it, so recess weeks and holidays are simply absent rather than estimated.
Clashing classesWhen two classes overlap it asks which to keep — once per clash, not once per week.
Skip what you likeUntick any class type you do not want — labs, tutorials, whatever.
RemindersSet how many minutes before each class you want a nudge, or turn them off.

Installing it manually

Only needed until the Chrome Web Store listing goes live. Works in Chrome and Edge.

  1. Download the codeFrom the GitHub repository, click Code then Download ZIP, and unzip it.
  2. Open your extensions pageGo to chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode, top right.
  3. Load itClick Load unpacked and choose the extension folder from what you unzipped.

Privacy

Short version: nothing you do here leaves your own browser.

Privacy policy
  • No data is collected, transmitted or stored by anyone. The extension has no server. There is nowhere for your information to go.
  • It never sees your password. It runs in a tab you have already logged into yourself, and has no access to the login page or your credentials.
  • It reads one thing: the timetable grid on your SUTD weekly schedule page — course codes, times, rooms and instructor names — and only while you have the panel open.
  • The calendar file is built on your own computer and saved straight to your Downloads folder. It is not uploaded anywhere.
  • The only thing it remembers is your reminder preference, in your browser's own extension storage.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no third-party code.
  • Access is limited to pages under sutd.edu.sg. It cannot run anywhere else.
